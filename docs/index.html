    What the check tells us

      Piped flow: relative dose vs flow

      Dose ∝ port suction ÷ flow (needle valve fixed), each design scaled to its own minimum. Flatter is better; below the red line, one needle setting stays within 0.3–0.8 mg/L.

      Handpump: water level at the spout vs flow

      Above the bore top the Venturi stays full; above +5.8 cm the Afridev overflows. Green band: both met.

      Port suction vs flow

      Suction draws the chlorine. At low flow it is set by the water column below the port (about 1 kPa).

      Bench: V1 dose vs flow

      Dark band: ideal 0.3–0.8 mg/L. Light band: acceptable 0.1–1.5 mg/L.
