    Piped flow: relative dose vs flow

    Dose ∝ port suction ÷ flow (needle valve fixed), each design scaled to its own minimum. Below the red line, one needle setting stays within 0.3–0.8 mg/L.

    Handpump: water level at the spout vs flow

    Above the bore top the Venturi stays full (good dosing); above +5.8 cm the Afridev overflows. Shaded: both conditions met.

    Port suction vs flow

    Suction drives the chlorine draw. At low flow it is set by the water column below the port (about 1 kPa).
