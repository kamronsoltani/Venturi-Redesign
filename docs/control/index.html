All cases

Columns are the pump flow rate (L/min) set for each run. Numbers in the cells are the result: how high water backs up above the inlet (cm). Lower is better. The overflow limit is about 8.5 cm on an Afridev and about 19 cm on an India Mark II at the normal 20–30 L/min. A negative level means the full pipe pulls a suction at the inlet; a real pump would draw air there (“air”). ✓ converged, ⚠ check. Hover or tap an assembly name to see its model. Tap a cell to fill the run form.
